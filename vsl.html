Why the cut rate matters

Shelby's 17-minute VSL produced 64 visual changes. Brook's 42-minute VSL produced 749. Per minute that is more than four times the cutting.

He is not talking to camera for long stretches. Every few seconds it moves to b-roll, a software demo, a review screenshot, a text card or a different framing. The captions are burned in and animated. The edit is doing as much work as the script.
