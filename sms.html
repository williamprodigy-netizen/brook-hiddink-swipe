The tell: it is the same script every cycle

Registering in June and again in July produced the identical four-message sequence from "Brook", word for word, only the name changed:

"hey is this Will? it's brook from high ticket" → "saw you grabbed a seat on my workshop later. you excited?" → "you planning on joining from your cell or laptop?" → "quick q before tonight - what made you sign up?" 21 June 2026
"hey is this Hans? it's brook hiddink" → "think you were just watching one of my ads and signed up for my workshop later - can you confirm you'll be attending?" → "you planning on joining from your cell or laptop?" → "quick q before tonight - what made you sign up?"21 July 2026

It reads personal and it is fully automated. The move worth stealing is the fourth message — asking what made them sign up. It is reply-bait dressed as service, and a reply is what keeps the thread deliverable.

The downsell ladder runs over SMS too

When the main program does not land, the same channel walks the lead down to a cheaper offer and then to payments.

  • “Hey Will, Brook here… Noticed you didn't grab the High Ticket Accelerator last week. No worries - I get it. Big commitment, right? That's why I created something a bit different…”
  • “Will - I'm writing this at 4 AM. Can't sleep because I know some good people are going to miss this. Tonight at midnight, I'm pulling the High Ticket Accelerator Lite forever.”
  • “Hey, still thinking about the high ticket ai accelerator? What if you spread payments over time? Start with as little as $295/month.”
  • “Will - 12 hours left to join High Ticket Accelerator Lite. Tomorrow you'll either wake up with access to my complete 'millionaire blueprint'... Or you'll wake up with”

Main program → High Ticket Accelerator Lite → $295/month. All of it by text, with a hard midnight deadline.

Retirees short $2,000 a month, parents missing their kids growing up, 9-5ers who dread Monday. Same offer, different pain, delivered to the same list.

The contrast with Shelby. She sends nothing by text between registration and her class — her pre-class channel is email only, 43 days straight. Brook barely emails and texts constantly as three people. They are near-exact inverses.
