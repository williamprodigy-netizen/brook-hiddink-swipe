Hey John, there are two types of people who start a business.

The first hits a problem and freezes.

They wait for someone to tell them what to do.

And they treat every obstacle like a stop sign.

The second hits the same problem and thinks "Okay, how do I solve this?"

The first type struggles for months or even years.

While the second type actually builds something that changes their life.

The difference?

One is still thinking like an employee.

The other became the CEO in their mind.

And I don't mean that in some fluffy, inspirational poster kind of way.

I mean it literally.

When you're an employee, someone gives you tasks.

They tell you what to do.

They set expectations.

And if you hit a problem you can't solve, you go to your boss.

That's how jobs work.

But when you run your own business?

There's no boss to go to.

You're the end of the line.

Problems don't get escalated.

They get solved by you.

Can't find a product you like? Figure it out.

Suppliers not responding to your emails? Find another way to reach them.

Store not looking right? Learn how to fix it or hire someone who can.

Ad account gets suspended? Find an alternative while you work on getting it back.

There's always a solution.

But no one is going to hand it to you.

And here's what I've noticed after working with thousands of students...

Our most successful students aren't 200 IQ geniuses.

They're not the most experienced people in e-commerce.

And they're not even the ones who ask the most questions...

They're the ones who try things first.

They hit a wall, attempt two or three solutions on their own, and THEN ask for help - saying "I tried X and Y, neither worked, what am I missing?"

Compare that to someone who hits the same wall and immediately asks "what should I do?"

That's a completely different energy.

And it produces completely different results.

So here's my challenge to you today...

Before you start any business - this one or any other - make the shift.

Stop waiting for someone to hand you the answers.

Start being the person who finds them.

Because that's the real difference between people who build freedom and people who just talk about building it.

It's not talent, luck, or circumstances.

It's ownership.

That's the mindset that builds businesses that create freedom.

And honestly?

That's the mindset that transfers to everything else in your life too.

So ask yourself one question...

Am I ready to own the problems, not just the wins?

If the answer is yes, you're already ahead of 90% of people who never make that shift.

Talk soon,

Brook
