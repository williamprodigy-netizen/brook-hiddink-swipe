Hey John, did you ever think why everyone talks about fear of failure?

"What if I try and it doesn't work?"

"What if I embarrass myself?"

"What if I lose money?"

These fears are real, and they stop a lot of people.

But here's what almost no one talks about...

Fear of success.

Think what if it actually works?

What if you start making $5K, $10K, $20K a month?

Because what changes then?

Everything.

Your identity shifts.

Your relationships improve.

People will look at you differently.

And that's terrifying for a lot of people - even if they don't realize it.

So what do they do?

They sabotage.

They "forget" to send the supplier emails.

They find reasons to delay launching.

They stay stuck in research mode forever.

Or they tell themselves they need "just one more course" or "a bit more preparation."

Not because they're afraid it won't work...

But because deep down, they're afraid it will.

Look... success requires you to change.

It requires you to step into a version of yourself that doesn't exist yet.

And your brain - the part that's designed to keep you safe - sees that as a threat.

It would rather keep you stuck and comfortable than let you grow into someone new.

So it creates resistance...

Excuses that feel logical.

Delays that feel reasonable.

All to protect you from... becoming successful?

Sounds insane when you say it out loud.

But it happens all the time.

So here's my question for you today...

Is it really failure you're afraid of?

Or is it what happens if you actually succeed?

Because if it's the second one, you need to know something.

The version of you that exists on the other side of success?

That person is still you.

Just with more options...

More freedom.

And more ability to provide for the people you love.

That's not something to fear.

That's something to run toward, not away.

Talk soon,

Brook

P.S. Most people spend years "trying" while unconsciously making sure they never actually succeed. Don't be that person.
