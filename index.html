How his funnel runs

  • VSL path. highticket.io/freetraining serves a 42:46 VSL on a player that hides its file, then an application, a scheduling page, and a confirmation page stacked with case-study interviews.
  • Webinar path — a different business. A VIP opt-in, a bridge page selling a paid VIP upgrade before the event has even happened, a countdown thank-you page, then a live Zoom room. The pitch closes to a $995 direct checkout at 1orderaway.com/now, capped at 97 spots, with six bonuses stacked to a claimed $100k+, a money-back guarantee and a $500 cash rebate when you post your first sale over $1,000.
  • The two funnels connect only at the DQ. Fail the high-ticket application and you are routed into the $995 webinar offer. A downsell by design.
  • No-shows are auto-registered into an encore about 2.5 hours after the live room ends. Verified across two separate cycles.
  • The bridge-page upsell is the notable move — he monetises between registration and attendance.
